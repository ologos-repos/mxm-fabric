# Profile — Lakatosian research programme

For a repo investigating a hypothesis rather than building a product. Used by
`triadic-reality-theory`, `creation-cosmology-programmes`, `semantic-actualism`,
`hydrotectonic-flood-model`.

## Structural overlay

`03-solutions-baseline/` carries the **falsifiability ladder** as sub-numbering:

- `3.1-hypothesis/` — the **hard core**: commitments held immune *by methodological decision*,
  stated openly as such. Not a scientific claim and not offered as one.
- `3.2-theory/` — the **protective belt**: adjustable content, one directory per module.
  Each module declares its interface at `02-systems-baseline/2.4-interfaces/`.
- `3.3-prediction/` — **discriminators** and the **appraisal log**.

`02-systems-baseline/2.5-verification/` carries the discriminator standard. A discriminator is a
claim, stated **before** evaluation, that (1) forbids some observation, (2) which the rival
account permits or forbids differently — a claim both accounts forbid discriminates nothing,
(3) is evaluable against a defined sample, and (4) states its failure condition first.

## Tier discipline

The four conditions govern **Tier 3 claims only**. Applying them to Tier 2 interpretive work is
a category error. Building a coherent alternative reading of existing evidence is legitimate,
expected work while a programme is young — Lakatos requires budding programmes be treated
leniently. Label it `forbids: none` rather than avoiding it, so the appraisal can later separate
accommodation from forbidding content without slowing construction now.

## Working rules worth inheriting

Each exists because of a specific failure, not because it sounds rigorous.

1. **Declare before you test.** Fix falsification conditions before the run. A criterion set
   after seeing the output is accommodation whatever it is labelled.
2. **Confidence labels on every figure** — HIGH (verified primary) / MEDIUM (secondary with
   specifics) / LOW / UNCERTAIN. A number without a source is not yet usable in a claim.
3. **Report disputes, do not smooth them.** State the range and the disagreement rather than
   the convenient value. This includes disputes *within* a literature you are drawing on.
4. **State the rival at its strongest**, and apply that symmetrically — steelmanning only the
   opposing side while summarising your own is the same failure wearing different clothes.
5. **Ask what the step forbids.** If nothing, it is coherence work; log it as such.
6. **Record refuted variants of your own**, with the reason they died. A belt that shows no
   casualties is not a belt.
7. **Search every relevant literature before recording a verdict**, especially where a
   "requires time" or "requires slow process" identification is doing the work. Such
   identifications are routinely contested, and a mainstream reading treated as settled fact is
   the most common way a verdict goes wrong.
8. **Fold external results into this programme's own framing**, and state what it adds *and*
   what this work contributes back. Citation is not integration.
9. **Surface a flipped result before writing it down.** The check is never optional; acting
   unilaterally on its reversal is.
10. **Target the rival's conceded anomalies, claiming only use-novelty.** A fact is *use-novel*
    if it was not used in constructing the theory, so a conceded rival anomaly can ground a
    progressive step despite being known in advance. Requires: the rival concedes it in its own
    literature, the explanation derives from existing mechanism rather than being invented for
    the occasion, and the failure condition is stated in advance.
11. **Record access failures and escalate before settling for a secondary.** Log URL, status and
    date where the claim lives. Escalate: open-access mirror, **the direct PDF path rather than
    the HTML landing page**, supplementary files, preprint, citing literature, then a summary.
    A primary that is load-bearing for a Tier 3 claim **blocks that claim until read**.
12. **Computational work**: scripts are the record, notebooks are drafts. State model, sourced
    parameters, determinism and the run command in the file's own header. No fitted parameters
    without saying so. Negative controls stay, labelled. Say what the result does *not* resolve.
    Co-locate code with the claim it supports and commit them together.

## Appraisal

`3.3-prediction/appraisal.md` is a standing log answering: is this programme **progressive** or
**degenerating**, right now? Log every belt change with its effect on that verdict. Watch for
interpretive freedom concentrating on one axis — that is the shape degeneration takes before it
is obvious, and it is only visible if the log is kept honestly.
