# Vision: purpose and intended future

Ratified 2026-10-23 by the Principal Operator, approved as drafted (WP-MXK-0015). Drafted from this repository's own record.

## Purpose

MxM Fabric exists because a model is not enough. A capable model with no
posture, no constraints, and no memory discipline is a fluent liability.
The fabric is the governance plane for agentic AI ecosystems: the layer
that makes an agent's work answerable. For every action, who authorized
it. For every claim, what evidence supports it. For every result, who
accepted it. Those answers are enforced by the harness, not left to
convention.

The fabric serves operators and builders who run more than one agent
under one authority: a Principal Operator who holds terminal authority,
aides who act under bounded delegation, and review that challenges work
before acceptance. The aide holds no authority of its own; the will
comes from the operator.

## Intended future

- A kernel whose controls are not only specified and implemented but
  demonstrated in operation across the runtimes that consume it, with
  the evidence filed where anyone can inspect it.
- A complete construct under one roof: headless core, governed
  contracts, and consoles that can show everything and authorize
  nothing.
- A memory discipline in which durable knowledge is admitted, provenanced,
  and superseded under authority, never merely accumulated.
- An assurance practice (EAF/IFA) that grades the evidence behind
  AI-assisted claims as a matter of routine, with human acceptance kept
  distinct from automated scores.
- A published, citable record: the architecture, the papers, and the
  conformance evidence maturing in the open, with maturity claimed only
  where evidence supports it.

The end state is not a bigger platform. It is a fabric in which governed
execution is the default condition of agentic work, portable across
models, tools, and environments, including disconnected ones.
