# EAF functional requirements

**Source:** `02-systems-baseline/2.2-architecture/SPEC-MXM-EAF-0001.md` (section 10 traces here)  
**Source WP:** `04-work-packages/WP-MXM-EAF-0001/` (proposed)  
**Status:** Requirements of record for the EAF functional scope. Moved from
SPEC-MXM-EAF-0001 section 10 on 2026-10-23 so the traceable requirements sit in
this canonical home. Priority is encoded in the modal verb, matching the
priorities tabulated in the spec: Must = SHALL, Should = SHOULD, Could = MAY.

## EAF-001 — Register inquiry, scope, authority, and acceptance criteria

The EAF SHALL register each inquiry together with its scope, authority, and
acceptance criteria.

## EAF-002 — Classify claims by epistemic type

The EAF SHALL classify claims by epistemic type.

## EAF-003 — Record assumptions and source provenance

The EAF SHALL record assumptions and source provenance.

## EAF-004 — Select appropriate analytical methods

The EAF SHALL select analytical methods appropriate to the inquiry.

## EAF-005 — Preserve counterevidence and defeaters

The EAF SHALL preserve counterevidence and defeaters.

## EAF-006 — Evaluate competing hypotheses against declared criteria

The EAF SHALL evaluate competing hypotheses against declared criteria.

## EAF-007 — Separate verification from human acceptance

The EAF SHALL separate verification from human acceptance.

## EAF-008 — Generate traceable dispositions

The EAF SHALL generate traceable dispositions.

## EAF-009 — Integrate with governed MxM memory interfaces

The EAF SHALL integrate with governed MxM memory interfaces.

## EAF-010 — Support domain profiles

The EAF SHALL support domain profiles.

## EAF-011 — Support probabilistic and sensitivity analysis

The EAF SHOULD support probabilistic and sensitivity analysis.

## EAF-012 — Support research-programme appraisal

The EAF SHOULD support research-programme appraisal.

## EAF-013 — Generate machine-readable assurance reports

The EAF SHOULD generate machine-readable assurance reports.

## EAF-014 — Support independent review

The EAF SHOULD support independent review.

## EAF-015 — Provide quality metrics and dashboards

The EAF MAY provide quality metrics and dashboards.

## EAF-016 — Record material transformations and input-output lineage

The EAF SHALL record material transformations and input-output lineage.

## EAF-017 — Detect and report material loss, distortion, misattribution, and unsupported additions

The EAF SHALL detect and report material loss, distortion, misattribution, and
unsupported additions.

## EAF-018 — Support justified information-theoretic measures with explicit models

The EAF SHOULD support justified information-theoretic measures with explicit
models.

## EAF-019 — Link fidelity assessment records to consequential dispositions

The EAF SHALL link fidelity assessment records to consequential dispositions.

## EAF-020 — Distinguish syntactic, statistical, semantic, and epistemic assurance

The EAF SHALL distinguish syntactic, statistical, semantic, and epistemic
assurance.
