# MxM narrative (companion to the diagram)

Most AI effort goes into building a better brain: a larger model, a longer
context window, sharper tools. MxM starts from the observation that a brain
is not enough. A brain with no posture (no will of its own, no constraints,
no memory discipline) doesn't become an assistant. It becomes a very fluent
liability. MxM, the multi-mode meta-harness, is the harness that gives the
brain a body worth trusting. Meta, because it governs the harness around
the model, not the model itself. Multi-mode, because one identity acts,
reviews, and remembers under the same posture. Six surfaces, loaded before
the work begins.

Authority comes first. The Principal Governance Triad splits three roles
across two kinds of mind. The Principal Operator is human and holds
terminal authority: nothing is decided without them. The Principal
Assistant is AI: it acts, carrying one identity across many workers. The
Principal Advisor is AI: it independently reviews the work and challenges
it. Authority flows one way, from operator to aide. The Advisor's challenge
travels back up to the operator, who decides. Beneath the triad sit the
Lead Operator and the Architecture Authority: they run the operation and
keep the architecture honest, but they are not peers of the triad.

Then the six surfaces. Three are always loaded: they are the aide's
posture, present before the first turn, and only the operator may change
them. The aide cannot revise its own posture mid-run. Mind is how it
reasons: a defined canon, terms defined, assumptions questioned,
conclusions updated on evidence. Morals is what constrains it, in three
parts: prohibitions (hard stops the aide must never cross), permissions
(trust scope: what it may touch, and the rule that no channel outranks the
operator), and attribution (every work product labeled under one rule:
Human-Curated, AI-Enabled). Mission is why it exists: identity, scope,
role. An aide, not a peer.

Three more are pointer-shaped: loaded when relevant, not carried
everywhere. Memory is what persists: a durable protocol with auto-flush,
so decisions outlive sessions. Methods is how work is done well: numbered,
reusable entries, deterministic scripts preferred over judgment. Means is
what it acts through: tools, agents, skills, channels, harnesses,
infrastructure.

All of it loads ahead of the work chain. A Work Package frames the job; a
WorkUnit scopes it; a Run executes; Evidence is retained and independently
reviewed; Acceptance is the operator's act; Integration lands it;
Disposition closes it out. Each step is verified before the next begins.

The whole framework compresses to one line: the aide holds no authority
of its own. The will comes from the operator.
